---
name: Supplier quote comparisons
description: Currency and cost boundaries for comparing supplier offers.
---

Supplier quotes must remain paired with their stated source currency. Comparisons should not imply a cross-currency ranking or landed cost unless exchange rates, freight, and allocation rules are sourced and defined.

**Why:** no exchange-rate or logistics-cost source is configured, so converting quotes would create false precision.

**How to apply:** Compare quoted unit price only within the same currency, while showing MOQ, lead time, validity, and terms separately. Add FX and landed-cost handling only when the business defines reliable inputs and rules.
