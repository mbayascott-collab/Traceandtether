---
name: Single-business resale model
description: The business ownership boundary for products and suppliers.
---

The business imports mainly from China and resells its own products in Zambia. It is not a multi-vendor marketplace, and suppliers must not register and sell independently.

**Why:** the user identified this as a core business boundary in the project brief; changing it would alter product ownership, pricing, and inventory workflows.

**How to apply:** Keep suppliers in internal sourcing and purchasing records. Do not add independent seller registration, vendor storefronts, or marketplace payouts unless the user explicitly changes this direction.
